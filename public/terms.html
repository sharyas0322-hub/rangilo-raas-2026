1. Website & Booking

This website provides event information, registration, ticket/pass booking, payment-verification and related digital services. Users must provide accurate name, mobile number, email and booking information.

2. Prices & Availability

Pass prices, quantities, dates, timings, venues and availability are displayed on the applicable booking page and may be updated by the organizer when required.

3. Promo Codes

Promo codes, where available, apply only according to the conditions shown on the booking page. A code may be limited by event, validity period, usage or other rules. Only one applicable promo code may be used per booking unless specifically stated otherwise.

4. Payment & Verification

Follow the payment instructions shown after booking creation. For payment verification, the customer may submit either the UTR/Transaction ID or a successful payment screenshot. Do not submit false payment information.

5. E-Ticket & Entry

Where an e-ticket/QR code is issued, it is linked to the booking details and may be checked at entry. The ticket should not be shared or duplicated for unauthorized entry.

6. Event Changes

Date, timing, venue, programme or pass arrangements may be changed when necessary. Customers should check the event information and follow official support instructions.

7. Misuse & Fraud

Fraudulent payment proofs, unauthorized access, multiple attempts to misuse promo codes, ticket duplication or interference with the booking system may result in cancellation of the affected booking.
