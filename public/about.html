Who We Are

SS Digital Solutions & Events is an event-support and digital services brand based in Patna, Bihar. We provide online event pages, registration, ticket/pass management, booking retrieval, e-ticket and QR-based entry workflows, and digital support for events and programmes.

Our booking platform is designed to make event registration simple, provide clear event information, and help organizers manage registrations and payment verification efficiently.

Current Event Projects

Rangilo Raas 2026 — Dandiya Night: a public Dandiya/Garba event in Patna with multiple pass categories and digital ticketing.

Ganga Devi Mahila Mahavidyalaya — Dandiya Night: a girls-only college event page with dedicated passes, booking and payment-verification flow.

For each event, the applicable date, venue, pass type, price and booking instructions are displayed before confirmation.
