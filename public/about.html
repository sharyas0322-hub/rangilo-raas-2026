Who We Are

SS Digital Solutions & Events provides website development, online registration and digital event-management support. We build and manage online pages and registration flows for events, college activities, sports tournaments and community programmes.

Our event websites may provide event information, participant registration, ticket/pass selection, online payment or payment-verification workflows, booking retrieval and e-ticket access.

What We Do

  • Website and web-page design/development
  • Online event and tournament registration
  • Event/ticket/pass registration management
  • College and community event digital support
  • Booking, e-ticket and QR-based entry workflows

Our Current Event Projects
