Information We Collect

Depending on the event and booking flow, we may collect name, mobile number, email address, event/pass details, booking ID, promo-code information, payment reference/UTR and payment-proof information such as a screenshot submitted for verification.

How We Use Information

Information is used to create and manage bookings, calculate applicable ticket amounts and discounts, verify payments, issue or retrieve e-tickets, manage event entry, provide customer support and maintain booking records.

Payment Credentials

We do not ask customers to provide UPI PINs, card PINs, passwords or OTPs through customer support. Payment processing is handled through the applicable payment service/provider.

Payment Proofs

When a customer submits a UTR/Transaction ID or payment screenshot, it may be reviewed by authorized event-support personnel for payment verification and fraud prevention.

Service Providers

Hosting, payment, messaging and other service providers may process information when necessary to operate the booking service, subject to their applicable policies.

Data Protection

Reasonable technical and administrative measures are used to protect booking information. Access is limited to people who need it for event operations, verification or support.
