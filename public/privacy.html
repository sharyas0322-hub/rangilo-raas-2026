Information We Collect

For event registration we may collect name, mobile number, email address, pass/registration details, payment reference details and information needed to verify a booking.

How We Use Information

Information is used to process registrations, verify payments, issue/retrieve tickets, provide customer support, manage event entry and maintain booking records.

Payment Information

Payment credentials such as card PIN, UPI PIN or OTP are not requested by us. Payment processing is handled through the applicable payment service/provider.

Data Protection

We use reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect booking information. Access is limited to people who need the information to operate or support the event.

Third Parties

Service providers such as payment, hosting or messaging providers may process information as necessary to provide their services, subject to their own policies.
