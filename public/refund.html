Refund / Cancellation Policy

For event tickets, passes and registrations

1. Before Payment

Please verify event name, date, venue, pass type, quantity, promo-code discount (if any) and final amount before making payment.

2. Customer Cancellation

Event tickets, passes and registration fees are generally non-refundable after successful booking, unless a refund is specifically approved under the applicable event terms or required by law.

3. Event Cancellation or Major Change

If an event is cancelled or a major change makes the booking unusable, the organizer may provide a refund, rescheduling option or another resolution as applicable. Customers will be informed through the contact details available to us.

4. Failed or Duplicate Payment

If money is debited but a booking is not created, or a duplicate payment is made, contact support with the Booking ID (if available) and payment reference/UTR. The transaction will be reviewed and the appropriate resolution communicated.

5. Invalid Payment Proof

A booking is not treated as paid merely because a payment screenshot or UTR is submitted. Payment proof is subject to verification. False or altered proofs may lead to cancellation.

6. Refund Processing

Where a refund is approved, it will normally be processed through the applicable payment channel or by another method communicated by the organizer/provider.
