1. Before Payment

Please verify the event name, date, venue, pass type, quantity and amount before completing payment.

2. Customer Cancellation

Event tickets/passes and registration fees are generally non-refundable after successful booking, unless a refund is specifically approved or required under the applicable event terms.

3. Event Cancellation or Major Change

If an event is cancelled or a material change makes the booking unusable, the organizer may provide a refund or another resolution according to the circumstances and applicable terms. Customers will be informed through the contact details available to us.

4. Failed / Duplicate Payment

If money is debited but a booking is not created, or a duplicate payment is made, contact support with the payment reference/transaction details. We will verify the transaction and coordinate the appropriate resolution.

5. Refund Processing

Where a refund is approved, it will normally be processed back through the applicable payment channel or as otherwise communicated by the organizer/provider.
